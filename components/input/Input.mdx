# Input

The **fields where you type or choose a value**. They all share one look: a label above, a 40px box and an optional message below.

**Not on this page:** checkbox, radio and switch are in <a href="./?path=/docs/app-components-selectors--selectors" target="_top">Selectors</a> · cells you edit inside a table are in <a href="./?path=/docs/app-components-tables--tables" target="_top">Tables</a> · the Chatbot and Accessibility assistant forms use these fields with their own colours (see those sections).

Every type has the same controls: **Tinted** (then **Colour**), **State**, **Label**, **Required / Optional**, **Text in the field** and a **Message** below (hint in grey or error in red, with its text). Every text control starts with the real text of the app: replace it to try your own. Open a story to see its **Accessibility** tab.

**Colour & background.** The colour is the colour of the section: blue (Facturación, IRPF) or orange (Gastos, IVA). It's set once on the form (`form--primary` / `form--attention`) or on a wrapper, and it colours the focus border, the checkboxes and the total. The **tint** is only for the mobile forms: they fill the screen with no card around them, so the tint shows where to type. Inside a card, fields are white.

## Amount

A figure in euros. It's a text field with `inputmode="decimal"`, so phones open the keypad with a comma. Two sizes (**Size** control):

- **Regular · 40px**, in forms (Base imponible en €): written like any text, **Sans, left-aligned**.
- **Small · 24px**, the two numbers you type inside the tax forms (Modelo 303 · 130); every other amount there is calculated. **Mono, right-aligned**, 160px wide, at the end of a row: `input input--sm input--num input--w-sm`. The **label is on the left** of the row. When it's **optional**, a checkbox comes before the label instead of "(opcional)": Aplicar compensación (Modelo 303). The required one is Importe ingresado en Hacienda (303 orange · 130 blue).

## Date

**Format DD/MM/AAAA.** The label carries the year selected in the year strip ("Fecha · 2026"): a date from another year is moved to that year.

## Description

Free text over several lines, always optional: the Notas of the tax forms (Notas del trimestre · Notas de la declaración). `<textarea class="input">`, at least 88px high, resizes vertically. The empty field shows a sentence in grey ("Criterios o decisiones de este trimestre..."). In the tax forms the box is **filled** in grey (`input--filled`: `--fill` background, `--line` border), so the notes read as a separate area of the form; use **Filled background** to switch it off.

## Mobile forms

Forms are lists of fields, 20px apart, in a set order. The app has them **only on mobile**, as a full-screen dialog (title, ghost Cancelar, the fields, a full-width Guardar stuck to the bottom). On desktop you type straight into the table. Choose the form in **Form**.

<Canvas of={InputStories.MobileForms} sourceState="none" />
<Controls of={InputStories.MobileForms} />

| Form | Colour | Fields, in order | Checkbox |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facturación · **Nueva factura nacional** | blue | Número de factura · Fecha · Cliente · Concepto · Base imponible en € · % de IVA | Aplicar retención IRPF del 15 % |
| Facturación · **Nueva factura exenta de impuestos** | blue | Número de factura · Fecha · Cliente · Concepto · Base imponible en € | — |
| Gastos · **Nuevo gasto** | orange | Número de factura (opcional) · Fecha · Proveedor · Concepto · Base imponible en € · % de IVA · **Total gasto** · Categoría IRPF | Incluir en el 303 |

**Nuevo gasto** opens from the Gastos list, which has two views: **Por cat. IRPF** (the form opens with that category chosen) and **Por % IVA** (it opens with that rate). Its rules:

- Seguridad Social / RETA → % de IVA 0 %, locked, with the hint "La cuota de autónomos no lleva IVA."
- IVA 0 % → "Incluir en el 303" off.
- **Total gasto** (`field__total`: Sans 14 / 700 caps, in the field colour) updates as you type the base.

## Accessibility

- Every field has a visible `<label for>`; the small amount with a checkbox has an `aria-label` instead.
- Required fields carry `aria-required="true"`; optional ones say "(opcional)" in the label, so it's read too.
- Hints and errors are linked with `aria-describedby`, so they're read when the field gets focus. Errors also set `aria-invalid="true"` and always say what's wrong in words.
- Focus is shown twice: the coloured border **plus** the yellow ring.
- Dates use `inputmode="numeric"`, amounts `inputmode="decimal"`: phones open the right keypad.
- The Total gasto is `aria-live="polite"`: screen readers read the new total.
