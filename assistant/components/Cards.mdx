# Question & form cards

<span className="review-chip">Under review</span>

When you ask the assistant to **add** something, the reply becomes interactive: first a **question** (if it's not clear what you want to add), then a **form** to fill in. They are built with components of the **main system** (Checkbox & radio, Input, Ghost and Big buttons) inside a reply.

## Question card

A reply with radio buttons: choosing one opens the right form underneath.

<Canvas of={Stories.Question} sourceState="none" />

## Form card

The same fields as the app's Nueva factura / Nuevo gasto. The colour follows the section: **blue** for an invoice (Facturación), **orange** for an expense (Gastos): the fields' tint and focus, the total, the checkboxes and the buttons.

<Canvas of={Stories.Form} />
<Controls of={Stories.Form} />

| Part | Built with |
|---|---|
| Width | the reply keeps `--ai-form-w` 560px (full width on mobile) |
| Intro "Rellena los datos y pulsa Guardar:" + title | reply text · `ai-form__title` 16 / 500 |
| Type of invoice (nacional / exenta) | **Checkbox & radio** in a `check-group` |
| Fields | **Input** · `form--primary` / `form--attention` + `form--tinted` (the tint of the section) |
| Error | **Input** error (`aria-invalid` + `field__error`) |
| Total gasto | `ai-form__total` · Mono 12 bold caps, section colour · updates while you type (`aria-live`) |
| Incluir en el 303 · Retención IRPF | **Checkbox** |
| Cancelar · Guardar | **Ghost button** · **Big button** with the `save` icon · `ai-form__actions` (Cancelar left, Guardar right) |

## After the form

Once saved (or cancelled), the form is replaced by a summary, so the conversation keeps a record of what happened.

<Canvas of={Stories.Done} sourceState="none" />

## Accessibility

- The form is named by its title (`aria-labelledby`); every field has a visible label.
- Errors are linked to their field (`aria-describedby`) and announced; the total is announced as it changes.
- Typing *cancelar* in the composer closes the form, like the Cancelar button.
