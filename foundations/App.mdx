## Principles

1. **Tokens only.** Components never contain a raw colour or pixel value, always `var(--token)`. Change a token and every component follows.
2. **Colour has a meaning.** Blue is **primary** (the main action) and **income**. Orange is **attention** (an action you must not skip, like Guardar) and **expense**. Green is the **brand**.
3. **Even spacing.** Ten spacing values on a 4px scale with a 2px half-step: 2 · 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 40 · 48.
4. **Accessible by default.** Text passes WCAG AA contrast, one yellow focus ring everywhere, every target at least 24 × 24px. **Don't rely on colour alone:** if a colour means something (paid, error, to pay), the words say it too.
