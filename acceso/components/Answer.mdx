## Accessibility

- When the answer appears, the focus moves to **Respuesta** (`tabindex="-1"`), so screen readers start reading it straight away, and keyboard users continue from there.
- The confirmation has `role="status"`: it's announced even if the focus is elsewhere.
- The bars are decorative: the text says what kind of message it is.
