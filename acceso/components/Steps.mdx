# Steps & options

<span className="review-chip">Under review</span>

Acceso fácil is a path of **steps**. Each step is one question with its **options**; choosing one adds the next step underneath. At the end of every screen, two **text links** let you go back.

Options are **toggle buttons** (`<button aria-pressed>`): the chosen one stays pressed. Min height `--af-option-h` 28px (**44px on mobile**), padding 2 / 12, 24px between options (12px on mobile).

## Steps

A step (`af-step`, `role="group"` named by its question) shows its question until you answer it. Then it shrinks to its **number** ("1.", "2.") and keeps the question for screen readers only (`is-done`): your path stays visible and short.

## Accessibility

- Each step is a `role="group"` named by its question, so screen readers say "¿Qué impuesto?, group" before the options.
- The chosen option says *pressed*; the number of a done step is `aria-hidden`, and the question stays as hidden text.
- The orange outline is not the only cue: the chosen option is also announced as pressed, and the other options turn grey.
